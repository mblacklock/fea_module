Equations and sign convention

C = (σₓ + σᵧ)/2  ;  A = (σₓ − σᵧ)/2

σₓ′ = C + A cos(2θ) + τₓᵧ sin(2θ)
σᵧ′ = C − A cos(2θ) − τₓᵧ sin(2θ)
τₓ′ᵧ′ = −A sin(2θ) + τₓᵧ cos(2θ)

Positive tensor shear acts in positive y on the positive x face. Clockwise individual-face shear plots above the horizontal axis; anticlockwise shear plots below it. X = (σₓ, −τₓᵧ), Y = (σᵧ, +τₓᵧ). Positive physical rotation is anticlockwise and corresponds to anticlockwise movement through 2θ around the circle.

Maximum shear here means maximum in-plane shear. The absolute maximum shear may differ when the zero through-thickness principal stress is included.
